## My product principles

- **Finish the job, not just the prompt.** A plausible response is not a verified business outcome.
- **Make agent authority explicit.** Use governed product APIs, permissions, evidence, and recovery rather than unconstrained actions.
- **Reserve people for real judgment.** Automate preparation; review uncertain or consequential decisions.
- **Optimize net value.** Include verification, exception handling, rework, and operating costs when measuring automation.
- **Cut scope deliberately.** Choose an actual buyer, repeatable problem, and smallest credible path to value.
